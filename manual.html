Console.

How to drive Console on the Akai MPC Sample: an 8-track tape with OP-1-style editing, up to 8 instruments (drum machines and synths) and a step sequencer. Controls as of November 2026.

Start and stop

Start
Switch the Sample on with the SD card in. A startup menu appears: PAD 1 = Console, PAD 2 = the normal MPC app. No answer in 15 seconds starts the normal MPC app.
Quit
Shift + STOP. Saves your song, quits and gives the Sample back to the normal MPC software.
Your songs
Projects, presets and settings are kept on the SD card, in a folder of their own, ConsoleData. Copy that folder to keep them safe. The Sample also keeps a copy, which refills the folder if the card ever loses it. With no card, Console uses the Sample's internal drive.
Update
Download the new zip. Drag the new autorun and Console folders onto the card and choose Replace, then switch on. Your songs are in ConsoleData, a separate folder, so they stay. The first start copies the new version across.
Remove
Put an empty file called UNINSTALL in the Console folder and switch on once. Console comes off the Sample and your songs stay on the card. Delete that file to install again. Or just delete the autorun folder.
If something goes wrong
Console/install-status.txt on the card says what happened. If Console doesn't get through starting, the normal MPC app starts at the next power-on.

Firmware. Console starts from the Sample's own SD card autorun feature. Don't update the Sample's firmware without checking that this still works with the new version.

The controls

Here is how the Sample's buttons, pads, knobs and encoder map onto Console. Each later chapter says what changes on its page. Most buttons have a second job with Shift held.

PLAY
Play / stop.
SEQ REC
Record.
STOP
While playing: stops playback. Otherwise: Back. Shift + STOP is always Back (from the top level it saves and quits).
LOOP
The tape's loop on / off.
B1
Instruments. Press again to go round an instrument's pages: sound, sequencer, automation, punch FX, then SONG, then back.
B2
Mixer. Press again for reverb, delay and the song inserts. Back leaves.
B3
The tape recorder. Press again: the selected track zoomed.
Pads 1-16
The 16 drum pads, or a synth's notes.
PAD BANK (latching)
Switch it on and the 16 pads play the picked drum chromatically, a semitone a pad. Switch it off and they are the 16 drums again. On a synth it gives the next 16 notes of the scale.
SEQ (latching)
The pads become the 16 step buttons. On the tape page they always are: steps 1-8 are tracks 1-8, and Shift + step arms a track.
Shift + SEQ
The pads become a step's settings (see Step settings). PAD BANK flips between settings 1-16 and 17-32. SEQ again goes back to the steps.
K1, K2, K3, FADER
The four knobs. Together they set the first four settings of whatever page you're on, and what those settings are changes from page to page (see Knobs, below). The fader is the fourth.
KNOB FX (latching)
Knob mode: pads 1-8 are knobs 1-8. Hold a pad and turn the encoder. Pads 9-16 are silent. With no pad held, the encoder is the jog wheel again.
Encoder turn / push
The jog wheel: turn to pick, push to choose. On the tape: moves the playhead a grid step; push goes back to the start.
MAIN VOLUME
Volume. It's absolute: the first time you touch it, Console is synced to the knob.
Sample Select
Menu: the project menu (songs, tempo, settings). On a sound page: the instrument menu. Shift + it: export.
PLUS / MINUS
The right / left arrows: the previous / next page of settings, steps 17-32 and on.
Shift + PLUS / MINUS
Octave on a sound page; pattern +/-. On the sequencer pages: Shift + MINUS = Undo, Shift + PLUS = Redo.
CHOP
Splice. Shift + CHOP: splice on every track.
ERASE, MUTE, TAP TEMPO, 16 LEVELS
Delete, Mute, Copy, Capture (the tape page uses some of them differently: see The tape).
PAD FX
A synth's pads page (key, scale, octave, velocity, curve). Press again to go back.
NOTE REPEAT
A synth's arpeggiator page. Press again to go back. Shift + NOTE REPEAT (labelled TRIPLET) turns triplets on / off.
SAMPLE
On an instrument's pages: back to the instrument's first page. Shift + it (INPUT CONFIG on the Sample): SETTINGS, from any page; Back leaves.
SAMPLE REC
Arms or disarms the selected track.
16 LEVELS
Off the tape page: Undo (the sequencer's too).
SHIFT
Shift.

Knobs

Console's pages have up to eight settings, and the Sample has four knobs, so the knobs work like this. In this manual a page's settings are numbered knob 1 to knob 8.

Knobs 1-4
K1, K2, K3 and the FADER, in that order.
Knobs 5-8
Switch KNOB FX on, hold pad 5, 6, 7 or 8 and turn the encoder. (Pads 1-4 work the same way if you'd rather use the encoder for all of them.) With no pad held, the encoder goes back to being the jog wheel.
Example: a sound page
K1 turns the first setting, K2 the second, K3 the third, the FADER the fourth. For the fifth, press KNOB FX, hold pad 5 and turn the encoder.
Example: the drum sequencer
K1 is the lane's LENGTH, K2 its RATE, K3 EUCLID, and the FADER its SHIFT.
Example: the tape
K1 is the selected track's volume, K2 its pan, and K3 does nothing.

Steps

Where a page says step button, switch SEQ on and use the pads: pad 1 is step 1, pad 16 is step 16. The pads printed with SONG, TIME CORRECT and PROJECT (Shift + pad 12, 14 and 16) are shortcuts: the song page, the next grid and the project menu.

The tape

Eight tracks. Record on a track, then edit it the way you would on a tape machine. B3 opens the tape.

Pads 1-8
On the tape page the pads are always the step buttons. They select a track. Press the selected track's pad again to zoom in on it. Selecting doesn't arm it.
Shift + pad / SAMPLE REC
Arm or disarm that track (SAMPLE REC: the selected track). Record only records armed tracks, so arm every track you want, for instance a drum and a synth together.
Shift + wheel
Scroll through the 8 tracks, four on screen at a time.
K1 / K2 / FADER
The selected track: K1 = volume, K2 = pan, FADER = volume (Shift + FADER: pan). K3 does nothing here.
SEQ REC
Start / stop recording on the selected track and any armed tracks.
PLAY
Play / stop.
Wheel
Turn: the playhead moves a grid step. Push: back to the start. Shift + push: the next grid (1/4, 1/8...).
MINUS / PLUS
Undo / Redo. Shift + them: zoom out / in.
CHOP
Split / join the box under the playhead. A splice shows as a thin divider between two boxes. Shift: every track.
ERASE
Cut: hold, turn the wheel to take several pieces, let go. It leaves an empty space. Shift + ERASE: Copy.
NOTE REPEAT
Drop (paste) what was copied or cut, at the playhead. Shift: drop and push the rest of the track later.
LOOP
Loop on / off. Shift + LOOP: the first press marks a loop point, the second sets the other (the earlier is IN, the later OUT). Both at the same spot: the points are cleared.
Shift + TAP TEMPO
Metronome on / off. It clicks while recording. Its volume: SETTINGS > METRONOME.
Shift + pad 12 / 14 / 16
As printed on the pads: SONG, the next grid (TIME CORRECT), PROJECT (the menu).

KNOB FX, SEQ, PAD BANK, PAD FX, TAP TEMPO and 16 LEVELS do nothing on the tape page.

The zoomed track

Press the selected track's pad again (or B3 again) to zoom in on it: its sound as a waveform, with a splice as an I-beam. Knobs work as described under Knobs, above. Back (or B3) returns to the tape.

Knob 1
Mono / stereo (about 5 clicks to switch).
Knob 2
The input: an instrument's drum, its kit, all kits. On a stereo track, the left side. About 5 clicks per choice.
Knob 3
A stereo track's right input.
Knobs 4-6
The box under the playhead: gain, fade in, fade out. Shift + touching knob 4 normalizes.

Instruments

B1 opens the INSTRUMENTS page: 8 slots, each a drum machine or a synth. A new project starts with every slot empty. Recording an instrument onto the tape uses the zoomed track's input (knob 2).

Wheel
Pick a slot. Push opens it. An empty slot: choose what goes in it.
Choosing an instrument
A picture of each instrument scrolls past as you turn the wheel (those without a picture show their name). Push to choose; it goes straight to its page.
Shift + push
On a loaded slot: change it or remove it.
Pads 1-8 (SEQ on)
Mute / unmute a slot.
B1 in an instrument
Goes round its own pages: sound, sequencer, automation, punch FX, then SONG. Back, from any of them: the instruments page.
EDIT (last in the list)
Every drum machine, then every synth, each with a tick box. Push to untick one and it no longer appears when you choose an instrument; tick it to bring it back. A song that already uses an unticked instrument keeps it.

Sampler

A drum machine of 16 drums. Each drum can load up to five samples. Push the wheel on a drum (on its sound page): SAMPLE 1, SAMPLE 2 and RANDOM; push a sample to browse, or ERASE + push to take it out. The knobs are LEVEL, PITCH, START, DECAY, PAN, REVERB, DELAY (shared by all its samples) and BLEND. BLEND all the way left plays only sample 1, the middle plays both at full volume, all the way right only sample 2. BLEND can be an LFO's target or a step lock, so it can morph between the two. RANDOM turns on samples 3, 4 and 5: each hit plays one of the drum's samples at random (BLEND is off while it's on).

MrSample

One sample played chromatically on the pads, with an amp envelope, filter and LFO. Push the wheel on its sound page to open the sample browser; each sample plays as you land on it. Push a sample to choose it, Back to leave. It is saved with the song.

Guitar

Your guitar, played live through Stompbox (amps, pedals, cabinets and effects). Plug it into the jacks on the back, choose GUITAR as an instrument and play: while GUITAR is loaded, Console switches the input to REAR for you, and puts it back to your own setting when GUITAR is gone. You hear it while you are on its pages. To record it, go to the tape, zoom a track, set its input to the guitar and arm it (it is heard while armed too).

The PRESETS page has Stompbox's 40 presets (clean, crunch, distortion, lead, bass, acoustic and effect sounds); the sound page has its amp, tone and cabinet knobs. INSERT 1 and 2 work after it as on any instrument. GUITAR has no patterns and no sequencer, automation, punch FX or song pages.

Stompbox is a separate plugin by Jacob Sabella and isn't included in Console. Put its Sample file, stompbox.so (64-bit ARM), in ConsoleData/plugins on the SD card. GUITAR appears in the instrument list once it is there.

Plug-ins

Console can host 64-bit ARM VST2 plug-ins built for the Sample: instruments appear in the instrument list, effects in the INSERT list. None are included; put each plug-in's .so file in ConsoleData/plugins on the SD card, and any folder it comes with (SAMPLES, ROMS and the like) next to it. They are copied to the internal drive when Console starts. A plug-in only shows up once its file is there.

Supported: MODEL-72, PROFIT-5, JN-106, STURM-TP, MONOMODULE, CLEMENTINE, OXZYGEN and the OP-1 Suite instruments (CLUSTER, D SYNTH, DBOX, DIGITAL, DNA, DR WAVE, FM, PHASE, PULSE, STRING, VOLTAGE) as instruments; STOMPBOX, VHS VIBES, BORIS and the OP-1 effects as inserts. The sound page shows the plug-in's first 48 knobs. The OP-1 effects carry their own presets; the OP-1 instruments have Console presets on the PRESETS page.

Presets

A preset is a sound saved for that one instrument (a Dexed patch, a drum kit, a Braids sound) along with its INSERT 1 and 2 effects. Press Sample Select on an instrument's sound page for the instrument menu.

SAVE PRESET
Name it on the naming screen. Saved for this instrument only.
LOAD PRESET
A list of that instrument's presets. Turn the wheel to pick, push to load. Its inserts come back too.
COPY / PASTE PATTERN
Copy the pattern and paste it onto another pattern, or onto another instrument of the same kind.
CLEAR PATTERN / CLEAR LOCKS
Empty the pattern, or only the step settings. Both ask ARE YOU SURE? first.

The naming screen

Used for songs, presets and renaming. The letter you're on is shown in a white box. Turn the wheel to change it; push to move to the next letter (Shift + push: the previous). ERASE erases a letter. PLAY saves the name, Back cancels.

A sound page

Press B1 on an instrument and you're on its sound page. The screen shows what the knobs do; the knob you're turning shows big.

Knobs 1-8
The settings on this page (K1-K3, FADER, then KNOB FX for the rest). While one turns, it shows big.
PLUS / MINUS
The next / previous page of settings. On a synth the wheel turns the pages too, one page a click.
Shift + PLUS / MINUS
The pads' octave (a synth's pads, and the chromatic drum pads). The screen shows OCT +1 and so on.
Shift + touch a knob
That setting goes back to its starting value.
Settings that pick a mode
DIST, CHOKE, a wave, on / off: it takes 5 clicks to move to the next choice, so you don't skip past. Shift + turn: one choice a click. Lists take 3 clicks a choice. Numbers (volume, velocity, tune...) take 3 clicks a step.
Pads
Play the drums, or the synth.
Sample Select
The INSTRUMENT MENU: presets, copy / paste / clear pattern, clear locks.
Reverb and delay
Each drum's REVERB and DELAY knobs set how much of that drum goes to the effect. The effects' own settings are on B2.

The PRESETS page (synths)

A synth's first sound page. The box shows the preset you're on. Push the wheel for the list, turn to hear presets as you land on them, push to keep one, Back for the sound you had. Knob 1 picks the bank (on OB-Xd, Dexed and others with more than one). Your own presets are marked *, the synth's own come next, then Console's (+) for synths that come with none.

LFOs

Every instrument has two LFOs per pattern. On a sound page press PLUS past the last page: LFO 1, again: LFO 2, then INSERT 1 and 2.

Knob 1 DEST
Which setting it moves: the instrument's, then its two inserts' number settings (shown I1 / I2 and the name).
Knob 2 WAVE
Sine, triangle, saw up / down, square, random.
Knob 3 SPEED
8 bars down to 1/32 of a beat, in time, with triplet speeds. Last: RND, a new speed every cycle.
Knob 4 DEPTH
How far it swings, -100 to +100 %.
Knob 5 PHASE
Where in the cycle it starts.
Knob 6 MODE
FREE (runs with the song) or TRIG (restarts at the pattern's first step).
Steps
The LFO's 16 steps (SEQ on). Tap: that step rests. Hold a step and turn knob 2 (its own WAVE) or knob 4 (its own DEPTH).

Drum sequencer

Every drum has its own sequencer: its own length, up to 64 steps, and its own speed. Switch SEQ on and the pads are the 16 steps; the screen shows what the knobs do.

Pads (SEQ off)
Pick the drum (its pad lights brighter) and hear it.
Pads (SEQ on)
Tap: the step on / off. While playing, the playhead moves along them. Steps inside the pattern light dim, steps with something on them bright, and steps past the end are off.
PLUS / MINUS
Steps 17-32, 33-48, 49-64 (for lanes longer than 16).
K1 LENGTH
The lane's number of steps (1-64).
K2 RATE
The lane's speed. Ten rates, slowest to fastest. It only changes the speed: the lane keeps its steps.
K3 EUCLID
Spread a number of hits evenly over the lane (OFF to switch it off).
FADER SHIFT
Turn the lane's pattern around by steps. (This is knob 4.)
Shift + PLUS / MINUS
Redo / Undo the last pattern edit.
Wheel
Push on an empty step puts it down. Push on a step that is on adds it to the selected steps (push it again: it leaves them). Back: none selected. ERASE takes the selected steps off (none selected: the step under the box). Leaving the sequencer page deselects.

Triplets

Shift + NOTE REPEAT (labelled TRIPLET), or MENU > SETTINGS > TRIPLETS, turns every 4th step off: steps 4, 8, 12 and 16 go dark and show as a dash, and the other steps play 3 in the time of 4. So a 1/16 lane plays 1/8 triplets. It works on every instrument at once. Steps already on the dark pads are kept, silent, and come back when it's off. The lane's RATE choices stay straight ones.

Recording into a pattern

With a sequencer, sound or automation page up, press SEQ REC while it plays and the pads you play go in. Turning a sound-page knob puts its value on every step as it plays, and it plays back as the steps go by (a filter sweep is heard as a sweep). Press SEQ REC again to stop. It doesn't touch the tape, and it turns itself off at Stop. SETTINGS > REC TIMING: FREE keeps your timing: a hit lands on the nearest step and the step's NUDGE is set to how early or late you played (drums: 10% of a step at a time, synth notes: 1%; half a step at most). GRID snaps to the steps.

Step settings

Velocity, chance, multiply, pulse, hold, jump, random, ramps, nudge, then the drum's own sound settings. They are set per step.

1. Pick the steps
SEQ on. Hold Shift and tap the steps you want. They blink.
2. Shift + SEQ
The pads are now the settings. Tap one: it's picked. Turn K1 to set it (K2 = its chance). Tap the same pad again: it's off.
3. SEQ
Back to the steps. Tap any step to finish.
Locking a sound setting
With steps picked and no setting pad chosen, the knobs are the instrument's sound page: turn one and that setting is locked on the selected steps. PLUS / MINUS: its other pages. Shift + touch a knob: its lock off. A * on screen means locked; a - means a setting that can't be locked.

The 32 settings, pad by pad

1 VELOCITY
How hard (1-127; all the way down: RND, random).
2 CHANCE
How often it plays: 100% down to 0%, or 1:2, 2:3... (the 1st of every 2 times round, and so on).
3 MULTIPLY
Hits inside the step: ×1 to ×8, or RND.
4 PULSE
Play the step again 1-9 times (or RND) before moving on.
5 HOLD
Wait 1-9 steps (or RND) after it, silent.
6 JUMP
Then go to step 1, 5, 9 or 13, skip one (+1), back one (-1), either (+/-), or RND. Once each time round the lane.
7 RANDOM
A random pitch each time: 2-6 notes over 1 or 3 octaves.
8 RAMP UP / 9 RAMP DOWN
The pitch goes up (or down) each time round: 2-6 notes over 1 or 3 octaves. One of RANDOM / RAMP UP / RAMP DOWN at a time.
Pads 10-32
The drum's own sound settings (TUNE, DECAY...): a parameter lock for these steps.
Chance for a component
With MULTIPLY, PULSE, HOLD, JUMP, RANDOM, RAMP or NUDGE set on a step, K2 sets how likely that component is each time round. When it doesn't happen the step just plays plain.

Pitch moves use the drum's TUNE (or PITCH) setting, so how far a semitone goes depends on the drum machine.

Synth sequencer

A synth's sequencer works on chords. The 16 pads (SEQ on) are the steps; PLUS / MINUS for more. Knob 3 chooses how the pattern is built.

UNIFIED
One sequence of chords. Every step holds a chord, and the whole pattern shares one LENGTH and one RATE. Easiest: it works like a normal step sequencer, and a chord is several notes on one step.
PER NOTE
Every pitch gets its own row. Tap a note to pick it: the 16 steps now show only that note. Each note has its own LENGTH, RATE, and its own pulse / hold / jump / chance. Every note starts at 16 steps.
Knobs (no step held)
1 length, 2 rate, 3 type, 4 the lit note's own length, 5 key, 6 scale.
Steps
Tap: the notes on / off. Hold a step: the pads light its notes; tap pads to add or remove notes. Hold a step and tap a later step: that is where the notes end (also across pages).
Shift + steps
Select steps, as for drums. VELOCITY, CHANCE, MULTIPLY, NUDGE (a step early or late, -50 to +50 %), PULSE, HOLD, JUMP (PER NOTE only), RANDOM, RAMP UP, RAMP DOWN, then the synth's own settings as locks.
Record
The notes you play go in, lasting as long as you hold the pad. Stop lets every sounding note go.

Why use PER NOTE

Polyrhythm
Give a low C a length of 3 and a high G a length of 4: they drift against each other and only line up every 12 steps.
Drone under a melody
One note with a long length and a slow rate while a melody note runs 1/16 on top.
Arpeggio that evolves
One note per row, each with a different length (5, 7, 8, 11): the pattern takes a long time to repeat.
Per-note feel
Chance 50% on a high note only, a jump or hold on one pitch only.

Use UNIFIED when you want chords, a hook that is the same length all the way through, or the quickest way to write a loop.

Song mode

A list of rows, OP-XY style: each row picks a pattern for every instrument and says how many times it plays. With song mode on, PLAY starts at row 1 and goes through the rows, then round again. Press B1 until you reach SONG, or Shift + pad 12.

Pads (steps)
The rows. Press one past the end: a new row (a copy of the last). ERASE + step takes that row out.
Knobs 1-8
The row's pattern for instruments 1-8 (all the way down: -, that instrument plays nothing in that row).
Shift + knobs 1-8
The row's punch FX pattern for instruments 1-8.
Wheel
A white box goes from instrument to instrument, then to PLAYS (how many times the row plays, 1-16). Push the wheel to pick what the box is on; turning then changes its pattern (Shift + turn: its punch FX pattern). Push again to let go.
PLUS / MINUS
Song mode on / off. (The tape shows SONG in its top line while it's on.) Off: the picked pattern plays, as usual.

LFOs, automation, punch FX

Automation

B1 after the sequencer: what is locked on this pattern's steps, one setting at a time. Recorded knob moves show here (name + REC), and so do step locks and step settings. A recorded setting has its own length (knob 2) and loops at that length whatever the notes do. Between steps with different values it glides.

Knob 1
Go through the settings that are set on some step: step settings first, then the sound's. Each has its own colour on the pads.
Steps
Lit: that setting is on the step. Tap a lit step: it comes off. Tap an unlit step that plays a note: it gets the setting, with the value of the lit step before it. Hold a step: its value shows, and knob 1 changes it.
ERASE + step
Take that setting off the step. Shift + ERASE: off every step (it asks first).
Undo
Puts back what you took off or changed.

Punch FX

B1 after automation: a sequencer of OP-XY style punch-in effects on the instrument you're on, one lane per effect, each with its own length and rate. Each instrument has 16 punch patterns, apart from its note patterns, and song mode picks them per row. Pads 1-16 are the effects:

Pads 1-5
REPEAT 1/4, 1/8, 1/16, 1/32, 1/8T: the slice from when it starts, round and round.
Pad 6 REVERSE
The beat just gone, backwards.
Pad 7 TAPE STOP
Slows to a halt over a beat.
Pad 8 HALF SPEED
An octave down, slowed.
Pads 9-11
LOW PASS (sweeps down), HIGH PASS (sweeps up), RESO PEAK (a ringing wah).
Pad 12 BITCRUSH
Grit and lo-fi.
Pad 13 GATE 1/16
Chopped in sixteenths.
Pad 14 PAN SWING
The whole instrument sways left and right, once a beat.
Pad 15 ECHO THROW
Everything into the delay while it's on; the echoes ring on after.
Pad 16 KILL
Silence.
Tap / hold a pad
Tap: its lane (the steps show its steps). Hold pads: they play while held (several: stacked). Recording while it plays puts them on their lanes' steps.
Knobs 1, 2, 8
The lane's LENGTH and RATE; knob 8 is TIME SCALE (×1 to ×32), every lane that many times slower for long sweeps.
Shift + pad 1-16
Punch pattern 1-16.

They stack: one from each group plays at once (TIME: pads 1-8; FILTER: pads 9-11; BITCRUSH, GATE, PAN SWING, ECHO THROW and KILL each on their own).

Pads and arp

PAD FX: key, scale and feel

Press PAD FX on a synth for its PADS page. Press it again to go back. Knob 1 KEY, knob 2 SCALE (12 scales, or CHROMATIC), knob 3 OCTAVE. The pads then play only notes in that key and scale, and the root notes light white. Knob 4 VELOCITY: TOUCH (the pads play and record how hard you hit them) or a fixed velocity. Knob 5 CURVE: SOFT (easier to play loud), NORMAL or HARD. The synth sequencer's key and scale are the same ones. The 16 pads play 16 notes of the scale in order (pad 5 is the 5th note); PAD BANK gives the next 16.

A drum machine has a PADS page too, with no key or scale: knob 1 OCTAVE (the chromatic pads), knob 2 VELOCITY and knob 3 CURVE.

NOTE REPEAT: the arpeggiator

Press NOTE REPEAT on a synth for the ARP page (PLUS: ARP 2). Press it again to go back. The arp turns the notes you hold, and the chords in the sequencer, into a run of single notes. What you record is the notes you played, not the arp's.

Knob 1 ON
Arp on / off.
Knob 2 MODE
UP, DOWN, UP-DOWN, DOWN-UP, RANDOM, AS PLAYED, CHORD (every step plays all the notes).
Knob 3 OCT
1-4 octaves.
Knob 4 RATE
The ten rates, slowest to fastest. A sequenced chord arpeggiates for as long as it lasts, so give it a longer length, or a faster rate, to hear more than one note.
Knob 5 GATE
How long each note lasts, 10-200 % of a step.
Knob 6 HOLD
The notes keep going after you let go; new notes replace them.
Knob 7 STYLE
GALLOP, OFFBEAT, TRESILLO, SYNCOPATED, STUTTER laid over your rhythm (OFF: just your rhythm).
ARP 2
Knob 1 GLIDE (portamento between the notes; the synth has to answer it), knob 2 BEND (each note starts bent up or down and slides to its pitch).
Steps
The 16-step rhythm: tap a step to go note (white), accent (red, louder), rest (off).

FX and mixer

The reverb and delay sends and the insert effects are on each instrument before the tape, so they are recorded with the sound. The tape room has no effects. Each instrument has a reverb and delay send and two insert effects; two more global inserts can be added.

B2
The MIXER. Press again: reverb, delay, song insert 1, song insert 2. The wheel also turns to the next / previous page. Back: off.
MIXER
Knobs 1-8 are the instruments in slots 1-8. Turn a knob: that instrument's volume. Hold Shift and turn: its pan. Pads (SEQ on): mute that instrument; Shift + pad: solo it (again: everyone back). Volume and pan are saved with the song.
Reverb
SIZE, TONE, LEVEL, WIDTH (100%: full stereo; 0%: both sides the same, mono).
Delay
TIME, FEEDBACK, LEVEL.
SENDS page
On every instrument's sound pages, right after INSERT 2. Knob 1: its REVERB send, knob 2: its DELAY send, knobs 3 and 4: how much of SONG INSERT 1 and 2 it hears.
INSERT 1 / 2
The last two sound pages of an instrument (after LFO 2). Each instrument has two insert effects in a row, picked from 36 (filter, EQ, tape, reverbs, granular...). Push the wheel to open the picture picker: turn to browse, push to choose, Back to cancel. Knobs 1-8 are the effect's first eight settings. A Ducker ducks its instrument whenever drum 1 of another instrument is hit.
SONG INSERT 1 / 2
One effect for the whole song. Every loaded instrument gets its own copy of it (after its own INSERT 1 and 2), all following the settings on this page, and it is recorded with each instrument. A compressor or limiter here works on each instrument on its own, not on the mix as a whole. Heavy effects on many instruments add up.
Choosing an effect
On an INSERT or SONG INSERT page, push the wheel to browse. The last entry, EDIT, lists every effect with a tick box: push to untick one and it leaves the list. An effect already in use stays.

Menus, input, export

Menu (Sample Select)
The project menu: songs (new, open, save as), tempo, time signature, export format, settings. Wheel: pick, push: choose, Back: close.
Shift in the project menu
NEW PROJECT becomes DELETE PROJECT and SAVE PROJECT becomes RENAME PROJECT. Pick the project, then confirm ARE YOU SURE?.
Settings
Push the wheel on a setting to edit it (the value shows in a white box), turn to change, push again to let go. They include METRONOME (push: on / off, wheel: volume; also Shift + TAP TEMPO on the tape page), COUNT IN (1, 2 or 4 bars of clicks before a recording starts), REC TIMING, TRIPLETS and INPUT.
Shift + Sample Select
Export: a stereo mix and one file per track (wav, aiff, flac or m4a, as chosen in the project menu). They go to the SD card, in ConsoleData/exports, in a folder named for the song and the time. Take the card out and open that folder on your computer.
INPUT
MENU > SETTINGS > INPUT: REAR (the jacks on the back), MIC (the built-in microphone) or USB (what a computer plays to "MPC Sample Audio"). A track's input (zoomed track, knob 2) then offers that input's LEFT and RIGHT. Pick one for a mono track, both for stereo. You hear it while you arm the track. The REC GAIN knob on the back sets the level of REAR and MIC.

Sharing your work

Everything you make is a plain file on the SD card, so there's nothing to set up. Songs are in ConsoleData/projects and exports in ConsoleData/exports. Take the card out, put it in your computer and copy what you want. Copy the whole ConsoleData folder to back everything up.
