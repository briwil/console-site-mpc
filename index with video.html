An app for the Akai MPC Sample

CONSOLE.

An 8-track tape recorder and groovebox for the Akai MPC Sample.

  • Sequence eight drum and synth tracks.
  • Dozens of synths, drums, and inserts.
  • Parameter locks and step components.
  • Layout patterns in Song mode.
  • Record, splice, cut, and drop to eight tape tracks.
  • Export final bounces to the SD card.
Get started See how it works MPC Sample firmware 1.3.0 · November 2026

A synth's sound page on the MPC Sample.

The tape

Eight tracks. Real splices.

Record on a track, then edit it the way you would on a tape machine.

Arm and record

Tracks can be mono or stereo. Inputs can be individual drums or whole kits. 

Splice, copy, cut

Splice, lift, and drop where you want.
Multiple splices can be lifted and merged before being dropped to one track.

Loop and overdub

Set in and out points with Shift + LOOP, loop between them, and switch between replacing the tape and adding to it.

Bounce

Tracks can be exported as wav, aiff, flac, m4a and saved to the SD card. Stems and final mix are automatically exported.

Drum machines

The 16 pads are the drums. Press PAD BANK and the pads play the drum chromatically.

Synths

Scroll through pages of parameters, lfos, inserts, and arp

sampler

The sampler can load up to 5 samples per drum. Load two samples and use the blend parameter to fade between. Set to random and load up to 5 samples that play back randomly.

Sequencer

Up to 64 steps

Every instrument has its own sequencer. Every drum has its own sequencer. Every note can have its own sequencer.

Per-lane rate

Each drum runs at its own rate, length, and Euclidean fills.

Step COMPONENTS

Hold Shift, tap a step, then lock velocity, chance, hold, pulse or length on just that hit.

Triplets

One menu setting turns the grid into triplets. LFOs and the arp have triplet rates too.

LFOs and arp

Two LFOs per pattern and an arpeggiator.

Effects

What you hear is what you record

Each instrument has a reverb and delay send, as well as two insert effects.
Two more global inserts can be added, and everything gets recorded onto tape. Tape tracks can be mono or stereo, inputs can be individual drums or the whole kit or synth.
